Creative Web Developer

I'm a Creative Web Developer with over a year of hands-on experience building modern, performance-driven websites. I focus on creating digital experiences that are not only visually engaging but thoughtfully built to perform. Over time, I've had the opportunity to work on multiple real-world client projects, helping transform ideas into fully functional websites that support actual businesses. My core expertise lies in Shopify and WordPress, where I build scalable, user-friendly, and conversion-aware platforms.

I also completed a 3-month MERN Stack internship , contributing to a full-stack e-commerce project within a collaborative team environment. Starting my journey as a freelancer during college helped me develop strong ownership, client communication skills, and a practical, results-oriented mindset. I believe great websites should feel creative, interactive, and purposeful — not just exist, but truly work.
